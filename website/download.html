get the app.

PYNQ Process for Apple Silicon Macs. One zip, nothing else to install.

Download for Mac
macOS · Apple Silicon8.3 MBpinkProcess-Mac-arm64.zip
step 1

Download

Grab the zip. It holds one app, built for Apple Silicon.

step 2

Unzip

Double-click the zip. pinkProcess.app appears next to it. Drag it to Applications.

step 3

Open

The app is signed locally. If macOS won't open it, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and press Open Anyway.

step 4

Find boards

Press Find boards. The rest of the app opens once a PYNQ-Z2 is connected.

You'll need

A Mac with Apple Silicon and a PYNQ-Z2 kit on the same network.

No board yet?

The app opens to the start screen and waits there until it finds one.
