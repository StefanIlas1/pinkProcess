Assembled envelope 176 x 138 x 40 mm. Aperture 156 x 92 mm.
Designed for bare Waveshare 7inch HDMI LCD (H) 14628 and TUL PYNQ-Z2.
Rear tray provides PCB tracks, SD service opening and a retaining tab.
Use the existing heatsink. Keep passive vents clear. No dedicated fan bay.
The face comparison illustrates a charcoal top and rose sidewall finish.
A filament change after the front skin can achieve the two-tone appearance.

Print fit coupons first, in PETG at 100%. Print the front face down and
rear tray with exterior face on the bed. Inspect snap shelves and bridges
in slicer. Seat panel ears in front clips, never press on glass. Fit board
in rear rails with heatsink facing the screen, SD toward service cutout.
Bench-test HDMI and USB touch before closing. The narrow sides make
connector shells and cable bend radii critical; excess lead can leave via
the side gates. Check your actual LCD tab thickness, heatsink clearance
and HDMI plug orientation. The side gates can be moved in model.py.
The compact depth and passive cooling need loaded thermal testing.
The STEP and STLs contain the enclosure only. No physical fit test performed.
