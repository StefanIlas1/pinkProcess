# pinkProcess product acceptance

A Mac user can discover the paired PYNQ-Z2, query a resident quantized analytical model on imported or live data, inspect its answer and model version, and change or restore its weights. A usable queue warning reaches the Mac at least one second before the first subsequent queue episode, within the ten-second prediction horizon.

Compare the two unchanged resident profiles with a request-rate variant of Service B and a full-pool rule. For the candidate replace the third Q8 feature with the larger of its original value and offered load over the preceding one second. Offered load is arrivals times the fixed 0.16-second mean hold time divided by capacity, capped at one. This uses the original Service B weights on the FPGA. Count arrivals only when a request first acquires or enqueues. Never count a handoff as an arrival.

Fresh runs use seeds 8301, 8302, 8303 with the previous traffic schedule. Replay five seconds of warmup, evaluate through 50 seconds, and sample once a second. Count one alert onset per consecutive warning streak; a busy queue or negative forecast breaks the streak. Credit only the first subsequent episode from the input timestamp, using the observed presentation delay to calculate remaining lead. A late arrival gets no early credit. Allow one credit per episode. Promote the candidate only if it delivers more one-second-early warnings than each installed profile and the full-pool rule in aggregate, improves over Service B in at least two runs, and has no additional aggregate false alerts versus Service B. Restore the starting board weights after testing. A failed candidate remains experimental.
